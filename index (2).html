ECHO VOID

Trapped in an abandoned dimension. Listen. Pulse. Escape.

HOW TO PLAY

A / D or ARROWS: move   SPACE / W / UP: jump (hold = higher)
SHIFT: sprint   E: ECHO pulse
P / ESC: pause   R: restart stage   M: sound

The void is dark. ECHO costs energy and reveals hidden platforms, secret stars and enemies for a few seconds - but wisps are drawn to the noise.
Collect every fragment (they recharge echo), touch checkpoints, stomp enemies from above, avoid spikes and pits. Then enter the portal. You have three hearts.
